What makes a great AiRise course

Every AiRise course follows the same rhythm, so learners always know what to expect.

  • One memorable model. Give learners a short framework they can use in the moment, like SHIFT or SOLVE.
  • Assess, learn, apply, plan. Start with a self-assessment, teach through real situations, apply it at work and end with a 90-day plan.
  • Keep teaching short and practice long. Most skills are learned by doing.
  • Use real and illustrative cases. Check real cases against original sources and mark composites as illustrative.
  • Accessible media. Videos under 15 minutes, with captions; documents as PDF.
  • Fair assessment. Section A tests knowledge; Section B tests application. Never publish answer keys.

How review works

  1. 1 · Submit

    Send your course from the review step.

  2. 2 · Quality review

    AiRise checks content, media and quiz against the guidelines.

  3. 3 · Changes or approval

    We reply within 5 working days with feedback or approval.

  4. 4 · Published

    Learners see it in their dashboard and can earn a certificate.

Accepted files

  • Video: MP4, MOV or WebM, up to 500 MB per file
  • Documents: PDF, Word or PowerPoint
  • Audio: MP3 or M4A
  • Cover: JPG, PNG or WebP, 16:9, under 5 MB
