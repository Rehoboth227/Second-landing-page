Certificates

Proof of skills,
not just attendance.

AiRise certificates show you completed the work and demonstrated competence through assessment. Every certificate has a unique number that employers can verify.

Three kinds of certificate

Earn as you go.

Course certificate

Pass the course quiz (14 out of 20 or more), complete the course checklist and meet attendance (for example 80% of facilitated sessions).

Programme certificate

Earn all nine course certificates and pass the final assessment (70 overall and at least 50% in each part).

Programme certificate with Distinction

As above, with 85 or more on the final assessment.

For employers

Check a certificate number.

Certificate numbers follow one scheme: AIR-C3-2026-001 is course 3, issued in 2026, serial 001. AIR-P-2026-001 is a programme certificate. Enter a number to read it, then contact AiRise to confirm it's genuine.
