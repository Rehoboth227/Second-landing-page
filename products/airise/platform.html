Platform and system design

One platform.
Three sides.

AiRise is a one-stop HRM platform. Talent learn and prove their skills, employers find and hire them, and the AiRise team connects the two, all on web and mobile.

How it fits together

The AiRise system.

Users and roles

Built for three tiers of users.

Talent · Tier 1

Students, graduates and experienced professionals building their employability.

  • Register, log in and verify by email
  • Update profile, bio, contacts and education
  • Upload required documents
  • Get a reference ID with an official headshot
  • Request opportunities
  • Learn soft skills
  • Take examinations
  • Schedule interview calls
  • Apply for jobs
  • Request reactivation for a career or job change
Employers · Tier 2

Start-ups and multinationals hiring people who need little or no training.

  • Register and log in
  • Assess the talent pool
  • Search by job heading
  • Advanced search by ranking and job heading
  • Cross-reference skills
  • Pay for targeted employee folder access
  • Request folders
  • Make a one-time employee request
  • Manage contracts
AiRise admin · Tier 3

AiRise's internal team, the mediator between talent and employers.

  • Access and manage the employee pool
  • Monitor each learner's growth
  • Send automated emails to people in the learning process
  • Proctor examinations
  • Organise interviews
  • Update rankings automatically
  • Talk to organisations in the chat room
  • Provide support in the chat room
Platform modules

Twelve building blocks.

The parts of the AiRise web and mobile platform, and what each one does.

Accounts and verification

Email or third-party sign-up, email verification, secure passwords.

Profiles and reference IDs

Bio, education, documents and headshot; a unique reference ID for each person.

Learning

The nine courses, about 140 modules, journals, trackers and 90-day plans.

Assessment and proctoring

Course quizzes, the four-part final assessment and practical role-plays.

Ranking engine

Turns results, skills and progress into a ranking by job heading.

Talent pool search

Search, filter and cross-reference skills for employers.

Folder access and payments

Pay for targeted candidate folders and one-time requests.

Interview scheduling

Book interview calls between talent, employers and AiRise.

Contracts

Agree and track the terms of each placement.

Chat and support

A chat room connecting employers, talent and AiRise admins.

Automated emails

Reminders and updates for everyone in the learning process.

Certificates and verification

Issue numbered certificates and let employers verify them.
