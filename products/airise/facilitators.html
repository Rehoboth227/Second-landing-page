A suggested workshop

Three hours, built around practice.

Every session starts with accountability and ends with a commitment. Most of the time goes to practice in pairs and small groups.

  1. 0:00
    Check-in

    Partners report on their 90-day plan and last week's practice.

  2. 0:20
    Teach and discuss

    Key ideas, the model and one case.

  3. 1:10
    Break
  4. 1:20
    Practice

    Activities, role-plays or conversations in small groups, with SBI feedback from observers.

  5. 2:30
    Plan

    Each learner commits to one action to try this week.

  6. 2:50
    Close

    A one-sentence reflection and next steps.

Facilitator guidance

What great sessions have in common.

Prepare

Read the course and the trainer notes, check real-world cases against original sources, and adapt names and examples to your learners.

Group size

About 12 to 25 learners, split into groups of 3 to 5 for activities.

Create safety

Agree ground rules on confidentiality, voluntary sharing and respect, especially for Self-Awareness, Teamwork and Work Ethics.

Insist on practice

Keep teaching short and practice long. Most skills are learned by doing.

Use accountability partners

Pair learners from the start; partners are central to follow-through.

Link to real work

Ask learners to bring a live situation to each session, with confidentiality respected.

Quick start

Before your first cohort.

Tick these off as you go. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Each learner needs

  • The course guide (Word or PDF) and a journal
  • A simple tracker for goals and weekly reviews
  • An accountability partner and, ideally, a mentor
  • Time in their calendar and a quiet place to reflect

Quality and safety

Handled with care.

Wellbeing

Self-Awareness includes reflective exercises. Facilitators are not therapists: keep sharing voluntary and know your referral routes.

Culture and context

Examples draw on Nigerian and global workplaces. Adapt names, norms and examples to your learners and avoid stereotypes.

Verify cases

Check real-world cases against original sources before teaching. Illustrative cases are composites, not real people.

Law and policy

References to law and workplace policy are general. Learners should check what applies where they work.
