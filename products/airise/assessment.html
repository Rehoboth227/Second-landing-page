Assessment

Show what you
can really do.

AiRise assessments check three things: do you understand the models, can you apply them to realistic situations, and do you use them in your own work?

Course quizzes

One quiz per course.

Taken at the end of each course, or within a week of finishing it.

Section A10 marks

Multiple choice on the course's models and ideas. 10 minutes, closed book, no devices.

Section B10 marks

Apply the course's model to a realistic scenario. 20 minutes.

Pass mark14 / 20

18–20 is a pass with distinction. Below 14, you get feedback and can re-sit within four to six weeks.

How scenario answers are marked

  • Use of the model0–3
  • Application to the scenario0–3
  • Judgement and reasoning0–2
  • Communication0–2

Markers mark against the criteria, not a single model answer. Concise answers that hit the criteria score fully.

Final assessment

Four parts.
100 marks.

Taken after all nine courses. You need 70 overall and at least 50% in each part.

Fair and transparent

The rules, up front.

Your own work

Answers must be your own. You can use generative AI to study during the courses, but not to answer any assessment or write your portfolio.

Accessible

Ask for adjustments in advance: extra time (25% is common), a reader or scribe, large print, a quiet room or language support.

Re-sits and appeals

Re-sit a quiz or a failed part within four to six weeks, up to two times. Ask for a re-mark within ten working days.

Your data

Results and portfolios are stored securely and used only as explained, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Results are returned within seven working days for quizzes and fourteen for the final assessment. Pass marks and timings are AiRise defaults and will be reviewed after the first cohort.
