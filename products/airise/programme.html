In every course

Six things you'll do
every time.

Every course follows the same rhythm, so you always know what to expect.

  1. 1

    Assess

    Complete the 12-statement self-assessment and compare it with an outside view.

  2. 2

    Learn and practise

    Work through the modules, practice activities and role-plays.

  3. 3

    Apply

    Use the course's model on a real situation at work or study.

  4. 4

    Plan

    Write a 90-day action plan with measurable goals.

  5. 5

    Stay accountable

    Agree a partner, track a few measures and review weekly.

  6. 6

    Reflect and complete

    Finish the course checklist and choose one habit to carry forward.

In the final module of the last course, you bring all nine models together in a personal operating manual and choose one habit per quarter to keep practising.

How each course is built

A working guide, not a textbook.

Each module has objectives, a worked example, a practice activity, a takeaway and check questions. Later courses reuse earlier tools, so by the end you're applying nine connected models.

Opening situation

A short real-life scene that shows why the skill matters.

Learning objectives

What you will be able to do after the module.

Core ideas and tables

Plain-language definitions, models, comparisons and checklists.

Worked examples

Real-world cases and composite workplace examples.

Practice activities

Tasks for individuals, pairs and groups.

Takeaway and "Ask yourself"

The one idea to use tomorrow, and a reflective prompt.

Check questions

Four questions per module, with hints.

Action planning

A 90-day plan with goals, partner, measures and review rhythm.

Measuring progress

Know where you stand.

Every course starts with the same kind of self-assessment, so you get a consistent baseline across all nine.

12 statements, rated 1 to 5

Four dimensions of three statements each. Each dimension scores 3 to 15, and the total runs from 12 to 60. Ask two or three people to rate you on the same statements, then re-take it after your 90-day plan.
