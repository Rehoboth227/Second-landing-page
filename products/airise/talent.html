Your journey

Eight steps
to your next role.

  1. 01

    Register

    Sign up with email or a third-party account, verify your email and set a password.

  2. 02

    Build your profile

    Add your bio, contacts and education, upload your documents and an official headshot. You get a reference ID.

  3. 03

    Learn

    Take the nine-course Employability Skills Series, at your own pace or in a cohort.

  4. 04

    Get assessed

    Course quizzes, a proctored final assessment and a practical role-play show what you can do.

  5. 05

    Get ranked

    Your results and skills place you in the AiRise talent pool, ranked by job heading.

  6. 06

    Interview

    AiRise schedules and organises interviews with employers looking for your skills.

  7. 07

    Get placed

    Internships and SIWES, graduate jobs and managerial roles with partner employers.

  8. 08

    Keep growing

    Request reactivation when you want a new role or a career change.
