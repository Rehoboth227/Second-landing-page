How it works

From sign-up to
your doorstep.

Six simple steps take you from a quick profile to a personal meal plan and fresh groceries every week.

  1. 01

    Register

    Sign up with email or Google / Facebook and verify your account in one click.

  2. 02

    Tell us about you

    Add your household, ages, health status and allergies.

  3. 03

    Get your meal plan

    A nutritionist builds a dynamic plan around your calorie and health needs.

  4. 04

    Receive it in 48 hours

    Your plan arrives by e-copy in your email and in the app.

  5. 05

    Order fresh groceries

    Shop straight from your meal plan, or by preference.

  6. 06

    Weekly delivery

    Farm-fresh produce delivered to your door every week.

Meal planning

Meal plans made for your body.

Request a plan for yourself or for everyone at home, including family members with special needs. Each plan is built on calorie needs and is dynamic, so it changes as you do.

  • Calorie and health-need based
  • Multiple plans per household
  • Pay by card or bank transfer
  • See which nutritionist picked up your plan
  • Delivered in under 48 hours
  • Review every plan

  • Updates by email and in the app
