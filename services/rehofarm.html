RehoFarm.
Growing Africa,
one harvest at a time.

RehoFarm is Rehohub's agricultural investment arm. We grow cash crops such as cocoa, rubber and oil palm to help the African economy recover, create jobs and reduce hunger, and our food crops supply GreenFed's meal plans and groceries.

How it works

From first talk
to harvest.

Six steps take a partnership from a first conversation to a crop in the ground and produce on its way to market.

  1. 01

    Talk to us

    Tell us whether you want to invest, partner, supply land or buy produce.

  2. 02

    Choose a crop

    Pick from cocoa, rubber or oil palm, or a mix, with the timelines laid out.

  3. 03

    Land and plan

    We secure suitable land and agree a planting and care plan.

  4. 04

    Plant and tend

    Local teams plant, weed, feed and protect the crop through every season.

  5. 05

    Harvest and check

    Each harvest is gathered and quality-checked before it leaves the farm.

  6. 06

    Sell and report

    Produce goes to buyers, processors and GreenFed, and you get regular updates.

Cash-crop investment

Long-term crops for long-term growth.

Cocoa, rubber and oil palm are perennial crops: once established, they produce for decades. RehoFarm structures investment in each plantation and manages the farm work, so the land, the jobs and the harvest keep growing together.

  • Cocoa, rubber and oil-palm plantations
  • Managed by RehoFarm teams
  • Local hiring on every farm
  • Harvests checked before sale
  • Regular progress updates
  • Produce sold to buyers and processors

Farm to table

Grown by RehoFarm, delivered by GreenFed.

Alongside cash crops, RehoFarm grows fresh food. It feeds GreenFed, Rehohub's nutrition service, whose nutritionists build meal plans and whose team gathers, checks, packs and delivers groceries every week.

  • Fresh produce for GreenFed meal plans
  • Quality-checked at the farm and again at GreenFed
  • Shorter supply chain, fresher food
