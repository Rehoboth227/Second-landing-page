Pricing

A guaranteed price before you book.

Like the ride-hailing apps you already use, every fare is a base fare plus distance and time, set by the vehicle your package needs. Heavier items add a weight charge, Express and Instant add a fixed amount, and peak hours add 25% to distance and time. Try it below: it's the same calculator AiMove uses.

How a delivery moves

From request to doorstep.

A nearby RehoTrans rider accepts your request, collects the package and follows the route to the recipient. You can follow every stage on the map in AiMove, and the recipient confirms delivery with a 4-digit PIN.
