About us

A conglomerate of
service providers.

Rehohub itself has zero products and zero goods. Instead, under Rehohub lie ten major subsidiaries that provide a variety of services and functions to the African market — with the intention to also access foreign markets.

Our market objective is total ownership of the services we render, minimising supplier and external influence so we can deliver premium services at market-minimal pricing.

Sustainability

Long-term thinking in every sector we operate in.

Innovation

Technology that expands what's possible for consumers.

Our mission

Mission

To create a one-stop solution hub, with sustainability and innovation at its core, expanding the frontier of technology and opening up opportunities to all consumers through market-minimal pricing and premium services.

Our vision
